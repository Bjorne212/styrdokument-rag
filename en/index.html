What is it?

Less time in PDFs, more time for the union

The board, presidium and leadership constantly need to know what the governing documents say: before a decision, when writing a motion, or when someone asks who is responsible for what. But the documents are written to be correct, not to be easy to search. LinTek’s archive is 99 PDFs and more than 900 pages, and the answer might be in the statutes, the regulations or a policy from several years ago.

Styrdokumentsboten does that searching for you. It retrieves the passages that best match the question and has a language model write an answer from them, and only them. Every answer shows which document and section it is based on, so it can be checked in seconds instead of half an hour of scrolling.

The bot reads the documents where the union already publishes them and keeps itself up to date. It stores no questions, has no accounts and runs entirely within the free tiers of GitHub and Cloudflare.
